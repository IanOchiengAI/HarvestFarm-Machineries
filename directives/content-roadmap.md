## 2. FAQ Section Content (To be added to Home/Shop pages)

**Q: Do you deliver outside Nakuru?**
A: Yes, we deliver to all 47 counties in Kenya. Delivery within Nakuru is often same-day. We use trusted transport partners for countrywide delivery within 24-48 hours.

**Q: How does the "Pay on Delivery" work?**
A: We require a small commitment fee before dispatch. You pay the remaining balance only after the machine has been delivered, inspected, and tested at your farm.

**Q: Do you provide installation and training?**
A: Yes! For complex machinery like Roller Mills and combined Posho Mills, our technicians handle the installation and will train you and your operators on safe, efficient usage.

**Q: Where can I get spare parts?**
A: We stock all genuine spare parts for our machinery at our Nakuru showroom. We can also courier parts to you anywhere in the country via G4S or Fargo Courier.

**Q: What does the 1-Year Warranty cover?**
A: Our warranty covers all manufacturing defects and engine faults. It does not cover normal wear-and-tear items (like belts and screens) or damage from improper use.

## 4. Video Content Strategy

Farmers buy what they can see working. Video is critical for trust.

- **The "Walkaround":** 2-minute videos of Ian explaining the key features of each machine in the showroom.
- **The "Demo":** Footage of the machine actually working (e.g., the posho mill grinding maize, showing the final sifted flour).
- **Customer Stories:** Short WhatsApp video clips of happy farmers using the machine on their farms.
- **Maintenance Shorts:** 60-second clips showing how to change a belt, clean a screen, or grease bearings.
